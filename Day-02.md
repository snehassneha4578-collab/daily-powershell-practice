# Day 2 — LinkedIn Profile Practice

Date: 03 October 2026

## Goal
Complete HackIndia's LinkedIn profile skill and document the learning.

## What I learned
- Set one clear LinkedIn profile goal.
- Improve the headline by showing what I build and relevant skills.
- Keep the profile focused on a specific target such as internships.
- Use action, tool, and proof when describing experience.
- Keep skills relevant to the target role.
- Avoid generic About-section statements.
- Ask for specific feedback instead of saying 'Please review my profile.'
- Include one specific change, why it was made, and a practical tip when posting a forum answer.

## LinkedIn profile goal
My LinkedIn profile should help recruiters shortlist me for electronics, embedded systems, VLSI, AI/ML, and software internships by clearly showing my technical skills, engineering projects, certifications, and hands-on development work.

## HackIndia forum answer
I completed my LinkedIn profile with a focus on internship opportunities. One specific change I made was improving my headline so it communicates my technical direction and skills instead of only describing me as a student.

I made this change because recruiters should be able to understand my career focus quickly when they first view my profile. I also aligned my projects and skills with the direction shown in my headline.

Tip for other builders: Avoid generic phrases like 'passionate learner' or 'looking for opportunities.' Clearly show what you build, the technologies you actually use, and the type of opportunity you are targeting.
